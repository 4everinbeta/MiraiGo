## Phase 10 Interaction Contracts

### 1) Provider source attribution visibility
- Every flight card displays `provider_label`.
- Provider availability panel remains visible before and after search.

### 2) Degraded-state messaging
- Partial success must keep available provider results visible.
- Explicit warning/status text is required when one provider fails or times out.

### 3) Freshness/provenance placeholders (Phase 10 scope only)
- Use only existing `provider_status[].reason` and top-level `warnings[]`.
- Do not introduce per-offer freshness timestamps in this phase.

### 4) Clarification continuity
- `turnSession` continuity is preserved across submit/answer/edit/continue.
- Continue CTA remains exactly `Continue to Recommendations`.
- Clarification controls stay disabled while requests are in flight.

### 5) Deterministic ordering presentation
- UI renders flight results in API-provided order (no client-side resort).
- Stable keys remain mandatory to avoid reorder jitter.

### 6) Accessibility and responsive status behavior
- Status indicators must include text labels, not color-only signals.
- Warning/degraded states should be announced (`aria-live="polite"` target).
- Provider availability cards stay 1-column mobile, `md:grid-cols-3` on medium+.
